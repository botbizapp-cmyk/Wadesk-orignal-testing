Key Features

A complete map of what WaDesk SaaS — the AI-Powered WhatsApp CRM, Marketing Automation, Chatbot Builder & Bulk Messaging Platform — can do. For each feature this page gives a short description of what it does, where to find it in the app, and — where it applies — whether it needs a paid plan. Every area links to its own detailed guide in the sidebar.

About the "Plan" column. Some features are only available on paid plans, and this is always enforced on the server. Some are simply on or off; others have a usage limit (for example, how many messages you can send per month). A dash means the feature is available on every plan. See Plan Enforcement for how this works.

Connectivity & Channels

How a workspace gets a WhatsApp number online. Everything channel-related lives on the Devices screen, which shows the setup for whichever engine the workspace is using.

FeatureWhat it does & where it lives
Three enginesConnect WhatsApp using the Unofficial API (QR pairing), the official WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta), or Twilio. Chosen per workspace on the Devices screen; the whole app adapts to the engine in use.
Unofficial API (QR pairing)Link any number like WhatsApp Web by scanning a QR code or entering a pairing code. Fastest start; no Meta approval needed.
WhatsApp Cloud APIConnect a verified WhatsApp Business Account, either by entering your Meta details or with one-click sign-up through Meta. Supports multiple numbers per workspace, each shown as a card on the Devices screen.
Twilio WhatsAppConnect one Twilio sender (your Twilio account details and sender number), with a test/live toggle. All sends then go through Twilio.
Device management & multiple numbersPair numbers, watch live connection status (Connected, Disconnected, Needs re-pair, or Failed), reconnect, and spread sends across several active devices — all on the Devices screen.
WhatsApp Coexistence (v1.1)Onboard a number that is already running on the WhatsApp Business app via Meta's one-click sign-up — keeping its chat history and mobile access. Messages typed in the app, the business's contacts, and past chat history are mirrored into your Team Inbox. Enabled by the admin under Settings; launched from the Devices screen.
Run several engines at once (v1.1)A workspace can connect any combination of the three engines simultaneously. Sender pickers across campaigns, broadcasts, scheduled sends, flows, and the inbox group senders by engine; the correct engine is chosen per message and replies go out on the channel the customer used.
Per-number proxy (IP isolation) (v1.2)Route each Unofficial-API number out through its own proxy IP, with a fail-closed preflight that verifies the outbound IP before connecting — so numbers on one server don't share an IP. Set per number on the Devices screen; lowers the IP-correlation behind bulk bans.

Full setup for all three engines: Connecting a Channel. Developer detail: WhatsApp Engines.

Multi-Channel New in v1.6

WaDesk is no longer WhatsApp-only. Connect the channels below and work them all from the same Team Inbox, with the same auto-reply, flows, AI agent and — where the channel allows — broadcasts. Each is added under Settings → Channels, scoped to your workspace, and replies back on its own transport automatically.

ChannelWhat you can doGuide

Facebook MessengerTwo-way Messenger chats with history backfill, auto-reply, flows, an AI agent, and broadcasts inside the 24-hour window.Facebook
TelegramConnect a bot for two-way chat, auto-reply, flows, an AI agent, and broadcasts to everyone who started the bot.Telegram
TikTokInsights, publishing, TikTok Shop and ad-lead capture into Contacts. DM inbox is wired end-to-end and activates once you have TikTok Messaging-Partner approval (region-gated).TikTok
SMS (Twilio & MSG91)Inbound texts and replies in the inbox, plus SMS broadcasts and SMS campaigns with real sent / delivered / failed analytics.SMS
LINE New in v1.7Connect a LINE Official Account for two-way chat, auto-reply, flows, an AI agent, rich menus, Flex messages and narrowcast broadcasts. Self-serve — no app review.LINE
WeChat New in v1.7Connect a Verified Service Account for two-way chat inside the 48-hour window, template messages, a custom menu, flows, an AI agent and mass sends.WeChat
Viber New in v1.7Paste one token and WaDesk registers the webhook for you. Two-way chat with read receipts, keyboards and carousels, auto-reply, flows, an AI agent and broadcasts to every subscriber.Viber
Email (MailTrixy) New in v1.7Bring your mailboxes into the same inbox as your chats. Reply from the inbox, import existing mail on link, sync on demand, plus email flows and email campaigns.Email

Each channel keeps its own message counts and analytics, so your reporting stays clean per channel.

Team Members & Roles

Overview

Each workspace is kept separate from the others. You invite teammates into a workspace and give each one a role that decides what they can see and do. Manage your team from Team Inbox → Members.

The Members page lists everyone with access to the current workspace, their role, their live status (online, away, or offline), and when they were invited and joined. From here you can invite teammates, change roles, and remove people. When you assign a role, the dropdown only offers the roles you are allowed to give.

Who can open this page: The Members page is for Admins and above (Admin and Owner). Agents and viewers who land here are sent back to the team inbox.

The Five Roles

Roles apply per workspace, not everywhere — being "Manager in Acme" does not make you a Manager in any other workspace. They form a strict order from most to least access (the rank number simply shows where each role sits in that order):

RoleRankSummary
Owner100Full access, including billing. The founder of the workspace.
Admin80Full workspace access and can invite members, but not billing.
Manager60Sees all team queues, can assign and resolve, manages tags and saved replies, views analytics.
Agent40Handles conversations on their team and the ones assigned to them; reply, note, resolve, snooze, tag.
Viewer20Read-only access to their team's queue and assigned conversations.
Legacy "member" role: Early installs had a role called "member". It is automatically treated as Agent, so existing accounts keep working with nothing to change.

What Each Permission Group Covers

WaDesk decides what each member can do across these areas. Wherever a member lacks a permission, the matching button or menu is simply hidden for them.

GroupWhat it controls
Inbox — viewingWho can see all teams' conversations, just their own team's, or only the ones assigned to them.
Inbox — actingReplying, adding notes, assigning, resolving, snoozing, tagging, changing priority, and bulk actions.
Workspace managementManaging teams, tags, routing, SLAs, saved replies, integrations, and workspace settings.
People & moneyInviting members, assigning roles, viewing analytics, and managing billing.

Inviting a Teammate

Click Invite teammate (only shown if you are allowed to invite people) to open the invite form.

  1. Enter the teammate's name and email.
  2. Choose a role — the dropdown only offers roles you are allowed to give (see below).
  3. Submit. WaDesk handles the email automatically (two cases below) and shows a result message.
CaseWhat happens
The email already has an accountThat person is added to your workspace immediately and counts as "joined" right away.
The email is newA new account is created with a random temporary password, shown to you once so you can pass it along. The person "joins" when they first log in.

WaDesk also tries to email the invite. If your install has no working email set up, the invite still succeeds and the temporary password is shown so you can onboard the teammate manually. New users are automatically taken to your workspace on their first login.

Caution: Inviting a member counts against your plan's seat limit, which is checked before the invite goes through. If you have reached the limit, the invite is blocked and you will need to upgrade or remove someone first. Inviting the same email into a workspace twice does not create a duplicate — you will be told they are already a member.

Who Can Grant Which Role

Assigning roles is controlled so no one can give away more access than they have. The dropdown you see when inviting or editing only offers the roles you are actually allowed to give, and the same rules are enforced again behind the scenes:

Target roleWho can grant it
OwnerOnly an Owner. Stops anyone from bypassing the founder.
AdminOnly an Owner. Admins cannot create other Admins.
Manager / Agent / ViewerAnyone allowed to invite or assign roles, as long as they are at least as senior as the role they are handing out.

Changing a role

  1. Find the member in the table and edit their role.
  2. Pick the new role from the dropdown (it only shows roles you can give).
  3. Save — the change takes effect and is recorded in the Activity Log.

Two safeguards apply: you cannot change your own role (to prevent locking yourself out), and the grant rules above are always re-checked — so no one can sneak in a role they are not entitled to give.

Removing a Member

Removing a member (which also requires permission to invite) removes them from the workspace and from any teams inside it. Two firm safeguards apply:

  • You cannot remove yourself from the workspace.
  • You cannot remove the workspace owner.
Audited: Every invite, role change, and removal is recorded in the workspace audit trail with who did it, who was affected, and the new role. Review these on the Activity Log page.

Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely cause & fix
The Members page redirects me awayYou are below Admin in this workspace. Only Admin and Owner can open it.
"Invite teammate" button is missingYou are not allowed to invite people (Manager and below). Ask an Owner or Admin to invite.
Invite blocked with a limit errorYou have reached your plan's seat limit. Upgrade the plan or remove a member first.
"Already a member"That email is already in this workspace. Edit their role instead of re-inviting.
Owner / Admin missing from the role dropdownOnly an Owner can grant Owner or Admin. If you are an Admin, you cannot create more Admins/Owners.
Invite email never arrivesThe install may have no working mailer. The invite still succeeds — copy the temporary password shown once and share it manually.
Cannot change my own role / remove myselfBy design, to prevent lockout. Have another Owner make the change.
Cannot remove a memberYou cannot remove the workspace owner. Transfer ownership first if you must.

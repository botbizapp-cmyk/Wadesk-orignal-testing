What You Get

WaDesk is a self-hosted, multi-tenant WhatsApp platform built with Laravel 12, Tailwind CSS, Alpine.js, and a bundled Node.js bridge. Here is a recap of the value covered across this documentation:

  • Three interchangeable engines: Connect WhatsApp via the Unofficial API (QR), the official WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta), or Twilio — without changing how the app works.
  • Shared Team Inbox: One WhatsApp number across your whole team, with assignment, internal notes, labels, and SLA tracking.
  • Marketing at scale: Bulk campaigns and broadcasts with audience targeting, pacing controls, and per-recipient delivery status.
  • Automation: A visual flow builder, auto-replies, WhatsApp template management with linting, WhatsApp Forms, and an embeddable chatbot widget.
  • AI Suite: AI chatbots trained on your own content and an AI call assistant, backed by pluggable OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google Gemini keys.
  • Growth tools: Contacts with custom attributes, Click-to-WhatsApp ads via Meta, analytics dashboards, and appointment booking.
  • SaaS-ready admin: Isolated workspaces, subscription plans with per-plan feature gating, free trials, coupons, wallet credits, and 30 payment gateways with multi-currency support.
  • White-label & secure: Per-platform and per-workspace branding, 2FA, role-based permissions, and an audit log of sensitive actions.
